From AI pilots to enterprise capability

Five transitions that help organisations move from experimentation to repeatable, governed AI capability.

Pilots are valuable because they reduce uncertainty. Problems appear when every pilot is treated as a separate technology experiment with no path into the enterprise operating model.

The transition to capability requires five connected moves: clarify the outcome, assess the starting point, architect the target capability, build a bounded implementation, and transform the surrounding operating model so the solution can be adopted and governed.

This sequence is deliberately iterative. What is learned during build may change the architecture; what is learned during adoption may change the process, controls or even the original business outcome.

The measure of progress is therefore not the number of pilots. It is whether the organisation becomes better able to select, design, govern, deploy and improve AI-enabled capabilities repeatedly.
