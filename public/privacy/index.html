Privacy

Privacy notice

A technical starter describing the intended verified-enquiry workflow. Adapt it to the actual legal entity, retention periods and applicable obligations before public launch.

Information submitted through the enquiry form

The form can collect name, email address, company, role, country, area of interest, message and consent choices, plus limited campaign/source information when present.

Email verification before permanent storage

The intended architecture does not insert an unverified enquiry into the D1 contact database. Submitted information is encrypted into an authenticated, expiring verification token. The permanent database write occurs only after the email owner follows the link and actively confirms the enquiry.

What verification means

Email verification demonstrates access to the submitted mailbox. It does not independently validate the person's identity, employer, role or claims in the enquiry.

Self-assessment

The public capability self-check runs in the browser and is designed not to transmit or store assessment responses.

Before publishing

This page is a technical starter, not legal advice. Replace it with a notice reflecting your registered business, actual service providers, retention, cross-border processing and marketing practices.
