Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how ZenAI Digital handles information you choose to send by email and how the public self-assessment works.

Email enquiries

The Contact page opens your email application so you can send an enquiry directly to info@cloudtechinfo.com. ZenAI Digital does not collect contact-form data through the website in this basic version.

How we use your information

Information you choose to send by email is used to review and respond to your enquiry and is handled through the normal email systems used by ZenAI Digital and CloudTechInfo.

Your choices

You decide what information to include in your email. A phone number is optional and only needs to be included if you would like us to call you.

Self-assessment

The public capability self-check runs in the browser and is designed not to transmit or store assessment responses.

Privacy review

This concise website notice should be reviewed against the final legal entity, email-provider terms, retention practices and applicable privacy obligations before broader commercial launch.
