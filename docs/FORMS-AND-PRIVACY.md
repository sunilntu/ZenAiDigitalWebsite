# Contact and privacy

ZenAI Digital currently uses a deliberately basic email contact workflow.

## Current workflow

1. A visitor opens the Contact page.
2. The visitor selects **Email ZenAI Digital**.
3. Their configured email application opens with `info@cloudtechinfo.com` as the recipient and `ZenAI Digital Enquiry` as the subject.
4. The visitor chooses what information to include and sends the email through their own email provider.

## Data handling

The website does not submit or store contact-form data in this version. Information is handled through the normal email systems used by the visitor and by ZenAI Digital / CloudTechInfo.

A phone number is optional; visitors can include one in the email if they want a callback.

## Future option

If business volume later justifies it, the Contact page can be upgraded to a managed web form with spam protection and a CRM or database integration without redesigning the rest of the site.
