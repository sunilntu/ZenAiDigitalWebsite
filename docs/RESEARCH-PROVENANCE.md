# Research and evidence provenance

ZenAI Digital V2 separates verified historical research from new AI-era extensions.

## Research foundation
The website links to the University of Technology Sydney record for Dr Sunil Mysore Kempegowda's PhD research and uses the Comprehensive Capability Maturity Model (CCMM) work as a research foundation for comprehensive enterprise capability thinking.

## AI-era extension
The ten-domain Enterprise AI Transformation Capability model in this starter is a contemporary ZenAI Digital proposition. It should not be described as if the ten AI-era domains themselves were validated by the 2018 PhD.

## External evidence links
The site configuration contains links to UTS, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, CloudTechInfo, YouTube and two Amazon short links supplied by the founder. Do not invent book titles, citation counts, follower/view counts, accreditation status or other metadata that has not been verified from the original source.

## Content principle
Use original research records and official profiles as evidence. Keep marketing interpretation clearly separate from what a source itself establishes.
